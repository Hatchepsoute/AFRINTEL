## Objective

This directory presents visual intelligence derived from the 40 incident cards documented by AFRINTEL in June 2026.

- **20 ransomware incidents**
- **20 data leaks or access sales**

- **20 African countries represented after expanding two multi-country records**

## Counting rules

Each incident card counts once in the global total. The two multi-country records count as two incidents, but they produce 15 African country exposures in the expanded geographic view. Palestine and Yemen are excluded from the African geographic view.

No substantiated double-claim involving the same victim was identified in the June cards. Repeated publications attributed to anisanas2 concern different Moroccan targets and are represented as continuity of source activity, not as a shared intrusion campaign.
