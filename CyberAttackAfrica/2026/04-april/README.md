# AFRINTEL CTI report - cyber incidents in Africa (April 2026)

This validated corpus contains **72 incidents or documented cases** first published or first observed by AFRINTEL in April 2026. The month assignment follows the publication or first documented observation date; a compromise date is not inferred when it is unavailable.

## Methodology

The figures are derived once from `victims_FR.md`, checked against `victims.md`, and reused in the reports, comparisons and STIX bundle. A multi-country incident counts once globally; country occurrences may therefore exceed the global total. The corpus measures documented AFRINTEL visibility, not the actual prevalence of cyberattacks.
