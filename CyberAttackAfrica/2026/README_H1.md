# AFRINTEL H1 2026 cyber threat report

From 1 January to 30 June 2026, AFRINTEL records **304 documented incidents or cases** in the validated monthly corpus.

## Methodology

The figures are derived from the French-first victim files, checked against the English files and reused in comparisons and STIX. Monthly assignment follows publication or first documented observation; no compromise date is inferred.
