## Methodology

Figures are derived from `victims_FR.md`, checked against `victims.md`, and reused in reports, comparisons and STIX bundles. A multi-country incident counts once globally; geographic breakdowns may follow a separate stated convention. The corpus measures documented AFRINTEL visibility, not the actual prevalence of cyberattacks.
