# CTI Report - June 2024: Moderate Ransomware Activity in Africa
👉🏾 [French version available here](./README_FR.md)
---

### 1. Executive summary

In June 2024, Africa recorded **3 documented ransomware victims**. The month saw a **decrease in activity** compared to May (8 victims) and the emergence of lesser‑known threat groups.

**Key figures:**
- 🔹 **3 victims** identified
- 🔹 **3 different groups**: ArcusMedia, Eldorado, Cactus
- 🔹 **Countries affected**: South Africa (2), Congo (1)
- 🔹 **Sectors**: Agriculture/agribusiness (1), Services (1), Legal services (1)

### 4. Key observations

- **Decreased activity**: only 3 attacks, the least active month since January 2024 (excluding months with no data).
- **Emerging groups**: Eldorado and Cactus are rarely documented; both appeared in 2024.
- **Unusual target**: a law firm (glynmarais) - a sector often less protected but holding sensitive data.
- **ArcusMedia presence**: already active in May (Egypt) and August (Mauritius), showing regular activity.
- **Central Africa**: Congo appears for the first time in the statistics.

---

### 5. Recommendations for June 2024

| Domain                        | Recommended action |
|-------------------------------|--------------------|
| Agricultural sector           | Raise ransomware awareness, back up production data. |
| Law firms                     | Encrypt client data, enforce MFA, train staff. |
| All organizations             | Monitor emerging groups (Eldorado, Cactus) and their TTPs. |
