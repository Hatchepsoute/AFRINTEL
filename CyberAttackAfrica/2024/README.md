# AFRINTEL Annual CTI Report - Cyber Threats in Africa - 2024

👉🏾 [Version française](./README_FR.md)

## 1. Executive summary

After full chronology, republication, and pending-case reassessment, AFRINTEL retains **119 canonical cyber incidents across 30 African countries in 2024**.

The previous 117 baseline is corrected by resolving two cases: **ACAO**, now attached to **26 July 2024** as a corroborated `Data Leak`, and **Misr Pharmacies**, attached to **30 December 2024** as `Data Leak / Claim - Unverified`. ACAO is not a net-new record compared with the earlier 128-record repository corpus; its November record had been temporarily removed while chronology was unresolved and is now moved to July. Misr Pharmacies is an additional resolved case based on the preserved original publication.

The landscape is dominated by **Ransomware: 91 (76.5%)**, followed by **Data Leak: 13 (10.9%)**, **System Intrusion: 7**, **Access Sale: 4**, **DDoS: 2**, **Defacement: 1**, and **Operational Fraud: 1**.

## 12. CTI analysis by type

Ransomware remains dominant. Data Leak now totals 13 with ACAO and Misr Pharmacies integrated under their actual evidence positions. System Intrusion is used where generic intrusion/access is better supported than ransomware or data exposure. Access Sale remains a claim category unless validity is verified.

## 13. Historical republications and duplicates

Seventeen historical/cross-year discoveries remain archived outside statistics. The March eTrade/eRIS duplicate remains excluded. ACAO is no longer pending.

## 14. Intelligence gaps

Initial vectors, exact technical compromise dates, complete claimed volumes, distinction between repost and re-exploitation, and public DFIR conclusions remain incomplete.

## 15. Recommendations

Use phishing-resistant MFA, PAM, segmentation, immutable backups, centralized telemetry, and strict CTI chronology.
