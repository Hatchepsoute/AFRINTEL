# AFRINTEL CTI report - first half of 2025

## Executive summary

AFRINTEL documented **94 incidents** during the first half of 2025: **58 ransomware** and **36 data leaks or access sales**. These figures represent documented public claims and publications, not confirmed incidents in every case.

The first-half picture shows the distribution of observed activity across countries, sectors and threat actors. The report is intended to support monitoring and defensive prioritisation.

## Methodology

The monthly victims.md files are the source of truth. Monthly STIX bundles were consolidated for this first-half view. Claims remain claims unless independently confirmed.
