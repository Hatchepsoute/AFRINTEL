# AFRINTEL - Threat Actor Intelligence

👉🏾 [**French version available here**](./README_FR.md)

This section contains AFRINTEL threat actor profiles and campaign-level
intelligence focused on threats affecting African organizations.

The analysis combines:

- threat actor profiling and victimology;
- MITRE ATT&CK TTP mapping;
- malware and tooling analysis;
- IOC and infrastructure correlation when available;
- incident and campaign timeline analysis;
- confidence-based attribution;
- distinction between observed, reported, assessed and inferred intelligence.

## Threat Actor Profiles

| Threat Actor | Type | Intelligence | Languages |
|---|---|---|---|
| NightSpire | Ransomware / Extortion | African victimology, TTPs, tooling, IOCs, attribution assessment | EN / FR |
| Akira | Ransomware / RaaS | TTPs, vulnerabilities, tooling, artifacts, detection-oriented intelligence | EN / FR |

## Analytical Principle

AFRINTEL does not automatically apply actor-level TTPs to individual
African victims.

Threat actor claims, technical observations, analytical assessments
and independently confirmed evidence are maintained separately to
avoid overstating attribution.
