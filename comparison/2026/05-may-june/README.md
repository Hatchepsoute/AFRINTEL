## Actor evolution

Databasehooligan led May with 8, 404Crew at 5, TheGentlemen at 4. June's top names were different: anisanas2 at 7, DeadLock at 4, LockBit 5.0 at 3.

## CTI assessment

June had fewer records overall but actually wider geographic coverage once multi-country entries are expanded. Morocco took over as the leading direct country label while Egypt and South Africa's concentrations dropped. The mix as a whole tilted back toward ransomware.

## SOC priorities

1. Maintain ransomware readiness, including tested backups, EDR coverage and privileged-access controls.
2. Prioritize public-sector and financial-sector monitoring.
3. Detect abnormal database exports, archive staging and outbound transfers.
4. Correlate repeated publications by victim, domain and date to prevent duplicate incident counting.

## Conclusion

June closed at 40 incidents against May's 103. Leaks and access sales dropped by 21 records; ransomware gained three.
