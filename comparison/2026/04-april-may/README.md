## Actor evolution

April was led by Grubder with 7 records, followed by Payload, APT73/BASHE and TheGentlemen with 4 each. May was led by Databasehooligan with 8, 404Crew with 5, TheGentlemen with 4 and NightSpire with 3.

## CTI assessment

The monthly total remained close, but the distribution changed. Egypt and South Africa replaced Morocco as the main geographic concentrations. Government / Administration increased by five records, while Education / University declined by five.

## SOC priorities

1. Prioritize public-sector identity, email and privileged-access monitoring.
2. Monitor HR and recruitment platforms for unusual exports, account creation and access patterns.
3. Alert on database extraction, archive staging and large outbound transfers.
4. Expand monitoring beyond the largest country concentrations because multi-country records increased from one to three.

## Conclusion

May recorded 57 incidents against 60 in April. The decrease came from ransomware, while leaks and access sales increased by one record.
