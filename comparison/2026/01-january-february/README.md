# AFRINTEL comparison: January and February 2026

## Comparable scope

This comparison uses the validated bilingual victim files: **30 records in January** and **27 records in February**. Each record is counted once in its publication or first documented observation month. January excludes the uncorroborated Kenyan “National Water Authority” claim; multi-country records are not duplicated.

## CTI reading

The corpus falls from 30 to 27 records, while ransomware rises from 18 to 20 and data leaks fall from 10 to 7. This is a change in AFRINTEL documented visibility and composition; it is not, by itself, evidence of a change in the underlying number of compromises.
